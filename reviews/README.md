# Reviews

> Structured reviews of high-signal external materials.

This directory contains distilled reviews of repositories, blogs, posts, and papers related to agents, prompts, workflows, evaluation, and practical AI tooling.

## Includes

- `repos/`  
  Reviews of open-source repositories and frameworks.

- `blogs/`  
  Reviews of technical blog posts and engineering writeups.

- `posts/`  
  Reviews of shorter high-value posts, threads, or public discussions.

- `papers/`  
  Reviews of papers with practical or conceptual relevance.

## Review goal

A review here is not a bookmark.

It should answer:

- What is it?
- Why does it matter?
- What can be reused or learned from it?
- What are its limits?

## What belongs here

Content in `reviews/` should be:

- condensed
- opinionated in a grounded way
- useful for future reuse
- stronger than a simple summary

## Rule of thumb

A good review should save future reading time while preserving the important signal.
